== Licence review (primary sources; summary)
Left out of everything public:
- EU NUTS regions (Eurostat GISCO, EuroGeographics): non-commercial use only. Removed from the lake, all training,
  eval and gold data (gold v1 342 -> 291 questions, gold v2 158 -> 137, dev v1 755 -> 686), and from training.
- 210 question/SQL pairs (36 scenarios) that OpenRouter served through Z.ai's own API: Z.ai's terms forbid using
  model output to train other models. They were in the v2, joint and RL training data (166 v2 pairs, 332 joint rows,
  18 and 42 RL questions by exact match); those sets are filtered (*_pub) and every model trained on them retrained.
- Gemma 270M runs (Gemma terms), smoke tests, raw generation archives, the MiniMax judge's free-text reasons.
Published with credit although no explicit licence was found: INPE fire hotspots, Ecobici GBFS feeds, IBGE
municipalities, NYC TLC. plan-v1-lfm350 ships the LFM Open License v1.0. Everything else: Apache-2.0 base models;
OGL v3, ODbL, CDLA-Permissive-2.0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY 2.5 India, Singapore ODL, PDL 1.0, Etalab 2.0, public domain.

== Notes
- gazet plan models (plan-*) were trained on Overture divisions and Natural Earth only and needed no retraining.
- evaluate.py now drops stored answers to questions that are no longer in a test set (it used to keep them).
- Training and evaluation code defaults to the public repos (finetune/*.py, passk.py, score_run.sh, to_gguf.sh,
  demo/app.py); new runs are uploaded there directly. demo/app.py served rl-q3-06b-lr2e5-pub until rl2 was redone; it now serves rl2-q3-06b-pub-lr1e5 (eval/rl2-q3-06b-pub-lr1e5/report.txt).
